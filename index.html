HOW TO PLAY

You get 8 cards. The top 5 are your 5-card hand and the bottom 3 are your 3-card hand. Tap one card, then another, to swap them. When you like both hands, press Draw.

Bets and pays

  • Your Total Bet is split evenly: half rides on the 5-card hand, half on the 3-card hand.
  • Each half is paid by its own table, so a Straight up top and 3-of-a-Kind below pays 2 + 16 times half your bet.
  • The 3-card hand always pays at least 1x (High Card), so you get that half back.
  • Win 10x your total bet or more for a Big Win.

Hands

  • All 2s are wild and count as any card, in either hand.
  • Jacks & Twos: a pair of Jacks, Queens, Kings or Aces. A wild 2 can make the pair.
  • Five of a kind pays as 4-of-a-Kind. Aces count high or low in straights.
